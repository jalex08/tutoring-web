On this page

    The short version

    • I collect only what you send me, plus basic statistics about visits to the site.
    • I use it to answer you and run your sessions. I never sell it or use it for ads.
    • You can ask me to see, correct or delete your information at any time: jose@josediaztutoring.com.

    Cookies and analytics

    I use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see which pages and buttons get used.

    • It sets two cookies on this site. They last up to 2 years and identify your browser, not you.
    • Google uses your IP address to estimate your approximate location (city level). Google Analytics doesn't log or store IP addresses.
    • Google signals and all advertising features are turned off. I never send your name, email or phone number to Analytics.
    • The site uses no other cookies, ad pixels or social-media trackers.

    Your choices: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

    Do Not Track

    Analytics measures visits over time on this site only. The site doesn't track you across other websites or show you ads, and I don't let other companies collect information here to follow you across other sites. For that reason, the site doesn't respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

    Children and students under 18

    • My services are for high school students, college students and their parents. The site isn't directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.
    • If the student is under 18, a parent or guardian should send the form or be copied on it.
    • If I learn that I collected information from a child under 13 without a parent's consent, I'll delete it.
    • Rules for in-person sessions and recordings are in the Terms of Service.

    AI tools

    I don't put students' names or contact details into AI tools. When I use AI to prepare practice material, I remove anything that identifies the student.

    How I protect it

    Every page uses HTTPS (with HSTS). Only I can access your information, and I chose my providers for their security. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever affects your information, I'll tell you, as the law requires.

    Your choices and requests

    Email me to:

    • see the information I have about you;
    • correct it;
    • delete it (except records I must keep for taxes);
    • stop any messages that aren't about your sessions.

    I reply within 30 days, and I may first ask you to confirm who you are. Making a request never changes the price or quality of your sessions.

    Links to other sites

    This site links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Google. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

    Changes to this policy

    When I update this policy, I change the "Last updated" date at the top. If the change is important, such as a new kind of information or a new provider, I'll also post a notice on the home page for 30 days.
