Accessibility statement

Last reviewed:

On this page

    I want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, magnification or other assistive technology.

    My goal

    This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. I check it with automated tools and by hand, and I fix problems as I find them.

    What I've done

    • Clear headings and page structure, in English and Spanish, each marked with its language.
    • Full keyboard navigation, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
    • Text descriptions for images, including the worked physics problem at the top of the home page.
    • Color contrast that meets Level AA.
    • An animation that stops if your device is set to reduce motion.
    • Text that zooms to 200% without breaking the layout.
    • Buttons and links large enough to tap.
    • Form fields with visible labels, and error messages that say how to fix the problem.

    Known limitations

    • Pages run by other companies, such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Google, aren't under my control.

    Need help?

    If any part of this site doesn't work for you, or you need its content in another format, contact me. Tell me which page and what happened, and I'll reply within 2 business days.
