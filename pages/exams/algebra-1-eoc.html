Questions parents ask

Can my child graduate without passing the EOC?

Yes, with an approved score on another test: SAT Math {{x:cut.sat}}, PSAT/NMSQT or PSAT 10 Math {{x:cut.psat}}, ACT Math or PreACT Secure Math {{x:cut.act}}, CLT or CLT10 Quantitative Reasoning {{x:cut.clt}}, or Level 3 on the Geometry EOC.

How many times can they retake it?

As many as it takes. It's offered in fall, winter, spring and summer windows.

Does the PERT still count?

No. It isn't on FDOE's current list of alternative scores.

When will we see the score?

Results reach the school within 24 hours of finishing the test.
