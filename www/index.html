    How to check its answers, catch its mistakes, and use it to study instead of to skip studying.

Questions

Do you tutor online or in person?

Both. Online sessions work from anywhere with a laptop or tablet. In-person sessions are available in Broward and Miami-Dade, at your home, a library or campus.

Are sessions in English or Spanish?

Whichever helps you learn. Many students switch between both, and exams stay in English, so we practice the vocabulary too.

Can parents get progress updates?

Yes. For high school students I can send parents a short update after each session: what we covered and what to practice.

Will you do my homework or graded work?

No. I'll help you understand how to solve similar problems so you can do graded work yourself. That's also what gets you through the exam.

What if I need to cancel?

Reschedule for free with at least 24 hours' notice. Sessions cancelled with less notice are charged in full.

My exam is next week. Is it too late?

No, but book soon. Exam weeks fill up first. A focused review on the topics that will be tested can still make a real difference.
